Storytelling for Impact

A nine module course on the brain science and skills of storytelling.

What this is

Nine self paced modules in Articulate Rise, an interactive workbook, and four interview videos. Members finish with one story of their own, built and rehearsed.

It runs today as SCORM inside a member learning platform.
